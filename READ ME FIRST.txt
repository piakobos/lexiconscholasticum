LEXICON SCHOLASTICUM - WINDOWS AND MAC
Updated 20 October 2026

Books now appear in compact rows. Filter by author or language; use Details
for descriptions and folder controls. Reading also has Warm / White paper,
Focus view and a compact word pane. Use Dark / Light in the top navigation
on any page in either edition. The choice is shared and remembered.
Study pages include subtle illuminated manuscript ornaments and refined typography.
Restart the app to load the updated interface.

One app includes both Lexicon Scholasticum and Romantica Lingua.
Click the L at the upper left to switch to Romantica; click R to return.
Romantica contains 621 shared reading entries, including 32 Spanish books and
collections by Santa Teresa de Ávila and San Juan de la Cruz. Select Spanish
and search Teresa or Juan de la Cruz. Books, poems, letters and minor works
are included, with searchable chapter navigation. Historical editions are
labelled and may retain scan/OCR errors.
Lexicon includes 129 shared Latin entries, with 104 additional Latin Library
works and all nine Ovid works available from that collection.
The Greek collection now has 205 entries, including 167 new texts:
Demosthenes, the other Attic orators, Thucydides, Theocritus and Theophrastus.
Find these in Greek · Classics; search by author or English title.
Use Book or part, Section or poem, and Find a section to navigate.
Original chapter boundaries are restored for 20 existing classical editions.
Other works use available headings and stable reading sections.
Passage numbers refer to reading chunks; source verse numbers remain in the text.

You only need the ZIP for the recipient's computer. Copy the ZIP to the recipient’s computer before extracting it. Do not run the app inside the ZIP.

WINDOWS
Extract Lexicon Scholasticum Windows.zip. Keep the entire extracted
Lexicon Scholasticum folder together and open Lexicon Scholasticum.exe.
Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is required; it is normally present on
Windows. No separate Python installation is needed.

MAC
Extract Lexicon Scholasticum Mac.zip. Move Lexicon Scholasticum.app into
Applications and open it. This contains the same bundled runtime setup
used by the previous working Mac package, for Intel and Apple Silicon.
No separate Python installation is needed. This is an unsigned app;
if macOS blocks it, use Privacy & Security > Open Anyway after attempting
to open it. The package was checked on Windows, not launch-tested on a Mac.

UPDATES
Close the old app before replacing it. On Mac, replace the app in
Applications. On Windows, replace the entire app folder with the newly
extracted folder. Personal libraries and progress are kept separately
in the user's account; do not delete those data folders.

The ZIPs include shared dictionaries, reading texts, fonts and source
credits. They include the explicitly requested reading additions, without saved bookmarks or progress.
First launch may take a few minutes to prepare the dictionaries/runtime.

READING DICTIONARY
Rest the pointer on a word for about 0.4 seconds to open its pane.
Click outside the pane, click X, or press Escape to close it.
