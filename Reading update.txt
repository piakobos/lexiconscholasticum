ENGLISH SOUND SPELLING QUIZ — 24 OCTOBER 2026

In Lexicon Scholasticum, open Quiz and choose English sounds. Choose Greek letters (without accents) or Hebrew letters (with vowel points). An English word appears; type its sound in the chosen alphabet. For example: hard → χαρδ / הָרְד. This is sound spelling, not a translation exercise.

134 offline English prompts, simple and extended word sets, pronunciation hints, on-screen Greek and Hebrew keyboards, Hebrew vowel-point keys, example answers, scores and missed-word rounds. Common variants are accepted, including pronunciations of hard with or without r. A guide explains this exercise's approximations. You can review and count another reasonable spelling when an automatic comparison does not match.

The original Latin and Greek verb quizzes remain under Verb forms. Switching activities keeps the current question in place. Dictionary and reading data, personal books and saved progress are preserved. Both delivery packages include the new activity.
